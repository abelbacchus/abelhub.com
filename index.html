Multazam, for Umrah

A counter for the circuits, and the duas to read while you walk them, all compiled into the app.

Captured on iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 26

1206 × 2622

What it needs

No signal
Every Arabic text and every translation is compiled into the app. Nothing is fetched, so it works in the Haram, in the air, and on a dead data plan.
No account
There is no sign-in, because there is nothing to sign in to. Your counts and your own duas stay on the phone.
No data collected
No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers. The app sends nothing anywhere.
iOS 17 and later
iPhone, portrait. The counters also run on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while a rite is in progress.

Not on the App Store yet

Multazam is still in testing. If you are going for Umrah and would use it, ask and I will add you to the beta.
