Count the circuits. Read as you walk.

An offline companion for Umrah, with every text inside the app.

Three screens

What is inside

The rites
Tawaf and Sa‘i, seven each, counted from the Black Stone and between Safa and Marwa. A counter bar follows you through the app, and the Lock Screen carries it too.
The library
Three hundred and five duas across sixteen categories. Each one names where it comes from, and the ones that are not from a hadith say so plainly.
The litanies
The Manzil, the Dala’il al-Khayrat and the Hizb al-A‘zam, read a portion at a time, each portion keeping its own place.
Your own duas
Up to twenty-five of your own texts, read on every circuit alongside the rest.

Where to get it

Multazam is in testing and is not on the App Store yet. If you would like to try it before it is, say so and I will add you to the beta.
