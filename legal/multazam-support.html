Support

What people ask about most, and where to write otherwise.

Start a conversation
The app stopped opening
Beta builds are signed for a fixed period and stop launching when it ends. Reinstalling the current beta fixes it; nothing you saved is lost by reinstalling.
The Lock Screen counter
Start it from Settings inside the app. It appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while a rite is in progress, and carries a button that advances the count while the phone stays locked.
My counts are gone
Counts live only on the phone. Deleting the app, or restoring a backup made before you started, clears them. There is no cloud copy to pull them back from.
A text looks wrong
Please say which dua and what you expected, and cite your copy if you have one. Corrections to the Arabic are the most useful thing anyone sends.
