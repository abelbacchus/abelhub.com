Privacy policy

No cookies, no analytics. A few companies still see your visit.

Effective 4 November 2026. This policy covers the website at abelhub.com. The Multazam app has its own policy.

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel, no cookie and no script that records what you do here. The pages are static files, and nothing you do on them is sent anywhere or stored in your browser.

What others see anyway

A web page cannot be delivered without someone handling the request. Three companies are involved in showing you this one:

  • GitHub hosts the site through GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and request details, and keeps them for security and abuse prevention under its own privacy statement. We never see those logs.
  • Google serves the two typefaces, from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Your browser requests them directly, so Google receives your IP address and the page you are on.
  • Your own network and whoever resolves DNS for you, as with any website.

If you write to us

Email to abel.bacchus@icloud.com is handled by Apple, and sits in a mailbox until it is dealt with. A support request goes through Atlassian, which holds what you type into it. In either case we keep what you send for as long as the conversation is useful, and we do not add you to a mailing list because there is not one.

Your rights

Because the site stores nothing about you, there is nothing here to access, correct or erase. For what GitHub, Google, Apple or Atlassian hold, their own policies and controls apply. To have an email exchange deleted from our side, write and ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to abel.bacchus@icloud.com.
