What it is
A studio of one. I design, write, test and ship everything myself, which is why the list of services is four lines and not forty.
What it makes

How I build
Plainly, and with as few moving parts as the job allows. Native Swift where it is an app, static files where a site will do, and infrastructure you could hand to someone else tomorrow.
What it does not do
Collect you. Nothing here runs analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and nothing has a login.
