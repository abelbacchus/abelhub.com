One person, one app.

The name the apps are published under. No agency behind it.

What it is
A studio of one. I design, write, test and ship the apps myself, which is why there is one of them and not twenty.
What it makes
iPhone apps for things people really do, starting with walking the circuits of a Tawaf. Each one tries to do a single thing without asking for an account.
How it builds
Native Swift and SwiftUI, no frameworks between the app and the phone, and no servers to go down. Where an app carries religious text, the text is checked against its source before it ships.
What it does not do
Collect you. Nothing here runs analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and nothing has a login.

Written and maintained by one person, in Canada.
