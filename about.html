One pair of hands, on purpose.

Small, deliberately. You deal with the person who writes the code.

Who
Abel Bacchus, in Canada. I write the code, run the infrastructure under it, and answer the email about it. Work reaches me at abel.bacchus@icloud.com, and the longer history is on LinkedIn.
What it is
Abelhub is a software studio run by one person. Everything is designed, written, tested and shipped by the same hands, which is why the service list is four lines and not forty.
What it makes
iPhone apps, and the cloud and automation work underneath them. Multazam is the app you can see; the AWS and pipeline work is the half that usually stays invisible.
How I build
Plainly, and with as few moving parts as the job allows. Native Swift where it is an app, static files where a site will do, and infrastructure you could hand to someone else tomorrow.
Our own app
Multazam is ours, and it is the clearest sample of the work. Its privacy policy and support page live here too.
What it does not do
Collect you. Nothing here runs analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and nothing has a login.

Written and maintained by one person, in Canada.
